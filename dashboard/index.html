What would change this view

  • Product detail after February 2025 showing merchant share jumping would mean MFS is already turning into a payments network.
  • Internet banking growth flattening would mean FY26 was a one-off, perhaps large business transfers moving online.
  • Interoperability between banks and MFS on equal terms would blur the contest; the winner is then whoever owns the customer's daily habit.

Sources

  • MFS product detail: Bangladesh Bank, MFS comparative summary statements, Dec 2021 to Feb 2025. The site returns no data after Feb 2025.
  • Channel totals: Bangladesh Bank, Monthly Economic Trends, Table IIG (Aug 2025 and Aug 2026 issues): annual from FY2014-15, monthly from Jul 2023 to Jun 2026.
  • Nagad's reporting gap: The Financial Express and BSS reports; it is located in the data by the agent count.
  • Provider shares: The Business Standard, from Bangladesh Bank's quarterly data, April to June 2026.

Data checks (all pass before export)

  • Months reported on two statement pages agree (0 disagreements).
  • MFS products add up to the total (within 1%, the rounding in the published lines).
  • The statement and Monthly Economic Trends agree on MFS value (75% of months within 0.1%).
  • Overlapping months agree across report issues.
  • Financial-year totals equal the sum of their months.
  • No missing months in either series.
  • The Nagad gap is located: MFS agents fall 23% in Mar 2025 and rise 36% in Mar 2026.

Limits

  • Product detail ends in February 2025. The mix after that is not public on Bangladesh Bank's site.
  • Internet banking value mixes retail and business payments; the falling average size suggests more retail use, but the split is not published.
  • Cash-out to merchants is part of "cash out", not "merchant payment".
